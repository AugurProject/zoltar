Explanation

The liquidity-provider position

An LP token represents a proportional claim on a pair’s Yes and No reserves.

How LP supply is scaled

Initial supply uses the smaller reserve, avoiding a geometric-mean multiplication that can overflow at raw share scales. After a permanent minimum lock, the initializer receives the remaining LP tokens. Ownership depends on the share of total LP supply.

What the token claims

A holder of wallet LP / total LP can withdraw that fraction of both reserves as raw Yes and No shares at any time, including after trading closes. Swap fees are taken from the input and stay in the reserves, and direct Yes or No donations immediately increase reserves, so both accrue to existing LPs.

Trades change the mix of Yes and No. Neither share redeems on an Invalid result. The provider holds Invalid insurance separately from the LP token.
