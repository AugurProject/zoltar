Explanation

Forks and resolution in Trading

When trading closes, holders can still withdraw their Yes and No reserves. What they can do with those shares depends on whether the question resolved or its universe forked.

What closes a pair

Swaps, initialization, and added liquidity close permanently at question end, when the universe forks, or when the QuestionForker exposes a final outcome. They are also blocked while the question ledger is not operational or is awaiting fork continuation. LP removal deliberately has none of those guards. tradingStatus() reports which condition applies.

After a fork

Withdrawing parent liquidity returns parent shares. Holders choose which child branches to migrate their shares into and supply liquidity to distinct child pairs. A child pair can open only if the question ledger returns to Operational before the question ends. A fork over this question occurs after its end, so only a fork over a different question can leave time for child trading. See Handle a universe fork for the steps.

After resolution

Settlement takes place at the question ledger. The pair only returns Yes and No shares, which can redeem there if they win. On an Invalid result, only the separately held Invalid shares redeem, so fee growth in the pair adds no redemption value. See Handle resolution for the steps.
