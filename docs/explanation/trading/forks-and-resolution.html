Forks and resolution in Trading

A pair closes when its market can no longer be traded safely, but it never traps a position. This page explains which events close it and what a holder does next.

What closes a pair

Swaps, initialization, and added liquidity close permanently at question end, when the universe forks, or when the SecurityPoolForker exposes a final outcome. They are also blocked while the SecurityPool is not operational or is awaiting fork continuation. LP removal deliberately has none of those guards. tradingStatus() reports which condition applies.

After a fork

A fork does not select a branch or migrate LP. A child pair can be initialized only if the child SecurityPool is back to Operational before the question's end date, which requires a fork caused by another question: pairs refuse initialization and swaps once the question has ended, and a fork caused by disputing this question always comes after its end date. Parent and child pools may share one ShareToken contract, but they have different universe-specific token IDs and different pair addresses. Handle a universe fork gives the step-by-step procedure.

After resolution

If the question resolves Invalid, the Invalid shares held in the wallet are the settlement insurance. Fee growth in the pair's Yes and No reserves creates no redemption value on an Invalid result. Handle resolution gives the procedure.
