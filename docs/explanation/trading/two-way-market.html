Explanation

The two-way market

Trading is the two-reserve constant-product design from How To Build: Augur Constant Product with Invalid Insurance, adapted to Statoblast. This page explains what happens inside one entry.

Mental model

A question ledger exposes the outcome shares for one question and universe. Creating a complete set mints equal Invalid, Yes, and No shares. Redeeming a set burns those shares and returns collateral at the question ledger’s current rate.

The pair holds Yes and No reserves. The router combines complete-set creation or redemption with a swap, then delivers the resulting assets to the user. The pair’s Invalid balance stays zero:

shareToken.balanceOf(pair,invalidTokenId)=0

Swaps and added liquidity are open only while the market is live; Forks and resolution lists what closes a pair. Parent and child question ledgers are distinct markets and therefore use distinct pairs.

One entry, step by step

The pair maintains k = Yes reserve × No reserve. Entering Yes looks like this; entering No is the mirror image.

Suppose the reserves are 1,000 Yes and 1,000 No, the fee is zero, and the trader mints 100 complete sets. Swapping 100 No yields about 90.909 Yes. The wallet receives about 190.909 Yes and exactly 100 Invalid; the reserves become about 909.091 Yes and exactly 1,100 No. See units and rounding for raw precision.

A No purchase simply reverses the reserves. No position record is created anywhere: wallet Yes, No, Invalid, and LP balances are the complete accounting state.
