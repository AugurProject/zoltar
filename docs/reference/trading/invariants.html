Trading invariants

These conditions are required at every successful external state transition. Reverting calls leave the prior state unchanged. Protocol-wide invariants are catalogued separately in Protocol invariants.

Pair custody

  • Yes and No reserves are the current ShareToken balances. Donations immediately increase reserves and accrue to existing LPs.
  • The pair's Invalid balance is exactly zero.
  • Transferring LP tokens moves the liquidity claim between holders and preserves total LP supply and pair reserves.
  • An LP withdrawal cannot claim more Yes or No shares than the pair owns. Withdrawal amounts are proportional to current balances and the liquidity burned.

Router custody

Every successful router operation ends with the same Invalid, Yes, and No balances with which that operation began. This deliberately preserves any pre-existing balances while preventing the operation from accumulating new share residue.

Swaps

  • For every successful exact-input or exact-output swap, k_after ≥ k_before, where k is the Yes balance multiplied by the No balance.
  • For unchanged reserves, an exact-input swap delivers the quoted output and reverts when the requested minimum exceeds that quote.
  • An exact-output swap delivers exactly the requested output amount.
  • An exact-output swap never charges more than the caller's maxAmountIn.

The product comparison starts from effective reserves so a donation cannot be mistaken for swap-created growth.

Lifecycle

  • Once the question has ended, the question has resolved, or the universe has forked, initialization, liquidity addition, and both swap forms revert permanently.
  • While fork continuation is pending or the question ledger is not operational, the same operations also revert for as long as that state lasts.
  • Proportional liquidity removal has no lifecycle guard.

Swap and price arithmetic

Comparison conditions
Use effective reserves and a fixed fee. Sequential swap comparisons assume no intervening trades, liquidity changes or donations.
Least sufficient input
The exact-output input quote is the least gross input whose exact-input quote meets or exceeds the target. One raw unit less yields less than the target or rounds below the minimum valid output. For reserves 1 and 4 with zero fees, a target of 1 costs 1, and that input buys 2 under exact-input rules. tradingArithmetic.test.ts.
Splitting a trade
Splitting a same-direction exact-input trade into two successful swaps cannot increase total output relative to a single trade with the same gross input. Each leg uses the updated reserves, including fees retained on the first leg. tradingArithmetic.test.ts.
Immediate reverse trade
Sending all output from a successful exact-input swap straight back through the same pair yields at most the original input. Equality can occur in fee-free, exactly divisible examples. auditTradingMathFuzz.test.ts.
Directional price symmetry
For the two directional spot prices rounded down in basis points, their sum is either 9,999 or 10,000. It is exactly 10,000 iff the unrounded basis-point price is integral. Multiplying both reserves by the same positive integer leaves both displayed prices unchanged if the scaled reserves fit. tradingArithmetic.test.ts.
Initial price rounding
On a successful initializer quote, reflecting the requested probability around 5,000 basis points exchanges the Yes and No amounts exactly. The resulting displayed Yes price rounds to the requested side of 50%, at or below the request for a request below 50%, and at or above it for a request above 50%. tradingArithmetic.test.ts.

Liquidity and position accounting

Backing per LP token
Let Y,N be effective reserves and S outstanding LP tokens. Across successful swaps, liquidity additions, removals and ordinary donations to an initialized pair, Y_after × N_after × S_before² ≥ Y_before × N_before × S_after². For a liquidity addition or removal, each reserve per outstanding LP token is individually nondecreasing. auditTradingMathFuzz.test.ts.
Permanently locked liquidity
Once a pair initializes, at least 1,000 LP units remain at the lock address under the protocol’s ordinary transfer assumptions. Returning all circulating liquidity cannot reset initialization or permit a second initial-price selection. Additional LP tokens sent to the lock can increase its balance. pairRouter.integration.test.ts.
Directional entry
A successful router entry that mints m complete sets sends the recipient m Invalid insurance shares and m + swapOut shares of the chosen direction. It sells exactly m opposite shares. Here m is the observed mint, which can differ from the cash amount. realCore.integration.test.ts.
Directional exit
To exit m complete sets by transfer, the router consumes m Invalid shares and exactly m + quoteExactOutput(m) long shares at the unchanged pre-swap reserves. Supplied long shares above that amount go to the designated refund recipient. Minimum cash and REP outputs apply independently to what redemption actually produces. realCore.integration.test.ts and pairRouter.integration.test.ts.
Predeployment shares and initial supply
Canonical Invalid, Yes and No shares sent to the predicted pair before deployment move to the share sink during construction. They buy no LP supply. At initialization the scale is exactly the smaller of the two supplied reserves. The designated LP recipient receives that scale less 1,000 locked LP units. pairRouter.integration.test.ts.

Executable evidence

Pair and router
Stateful swaps compare effective reserve products, outcome balances and router residue. Separate cases check exact-output bounds, proportional removal, counterfactual donations, temporary inactivity and permanent closure. pairRouter.integration.test.ts.
Arithmetic and collateral
Integer-model checks exercise trading math across generated inputs. Router collateral checks verify that calls leave no wrapped collateral or services allowance. auditTradingMathFuzz.test.ts, audit4TradingRouterCollateralResidue.test.ts.
