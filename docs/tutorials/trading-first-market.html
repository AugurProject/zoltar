Tutorial

Your first Trading market

In this tutorial you create and seed the first pair for an existing SecurityPool. You will finish with an initialized YES/NO pair, LP tokens, and separately held INVALID shares in your wallet.

Before you start

Build and open the standalone Trading UI on a supported network by following Configure the Trading UI. If the network's deterministic trading contracts are missing, the UI shows the deployment screen; complete Deploy the Trading contracts first. Fund a wallet on that network with enough ETH for the initial deposit and gas.

You also need a SecurityPool that can still be initialized: operational, unresolved, with a question that has not ended and a universe that has not forked. The UI checks this for you, as step 2 shows. The pool must also have a current valid REP price and enough minting capacity for the collateral your deposit creates.

The Minting capacity field on the pool's details shows used and ceiling ETH; the router simulation in step 4 is what confirms that your deposit fits. If it reports a stale price or insufficient capacity, do not submit: wait for a valid price and free capacity, or reduce the deposit.

Initialize the pair

  1. Open the live UI (no ?simulate=1), connect the wallet, and select the Create new market tab.
  2. The Security Pools list shows pools in the selected universe that have no trading market yet. Find the exact pool you want and select Details. Confirm the full address is the branch you intend, then look at the action button: if it reads Deploy trading pool, the pool is eligible; if it is disabled with a reason such as Question ended, Pool inactive, Awaiting fork continuation, or Universe forked, choose another pool. The Complete-set backing figure shown here can change as retention accrues; the router simulation in step 4 is authoritative for the rate your transaction will use. Select Deploy trading pool.
  3. The create-market form opens with the badge Pair not created and Pair reading Not deployed. In the Initialize / Add / Remove switch, select Initialize, then enter an ETH amount and a Conditional YES price above 0% and below 100%. One transaction will create the pair and seed it.
  4. Select Simulate liquidity transaction. The authoritative router simulation shows the complete-set shares created, YES and NO deposited, unused directional shares returned, INVALID retained, and expected LP tokens. Confirm the reserve direction matches the odds you intended.
  5. Select Submit liquidity transaction and confirm it in the wallet. The client refreshes the account, network, current block, and simulation before submitting; if any of them changed, simulate again.
  6. After confirmation, open the Market tab, select the pool, and verify that its badge reads Trading open, the Pair field shows an address, and the Conditional YES / NO bar shows the price you seeded (70% YES for the example below). Open Portfolio, select the same pool, and verify your new LP balance and the returned INVALID balance.

What you now hold

For a 70% Conditional YES price, the initialized reserves are roughly 30 YES to 70 NO. The NO reserve is the larger one because in a constant-product pair the opposite reserve sets the spot price; Conditional pricing explains the apparent reversal. Your LP tokens represent only the deposited YES and NO. The INVALID shares and any unused directional shares stay in your wallet as separate balances.
