Tutorial

Your first Trading market

You will open trading for a SecurityPool by creating its trading pool and seeding it at 70% Yes with 0.01 ETH. You finish with LP tokens and separately held Invalid shares in your wallet, and the market listed as open.

Before you start

Trading is the market interface for Statoblast: a trading pool swaps the Yes and No shares of exactly one SecurityPool, and a SecurityPool without one has no market yet. The UI also calls the trading pool a pair.

To practise without a wallet, run bun run app:serve:trading and open http://localhost:4163/?simulate=1&simScenario=deployed#/create-market/0x8e87c0e3028ef202D2188c11BBAB3dDAd097DBCC. That simulator scenario has one SecurityPool and no trading pool; the screenshots below come from it. For a live network, follow Configure the Trading UI, and if the UI opens the deployment screen, complete Deploy the Trading contracts first. Fund the wallet with the deposit plus gas.

Find a SecurityPool without a market

  1. Select the Create tab. Paste the SecurityPool address from Statoblast into Search pools and select Open pool. Opening it saves a favorite; the list shows your favorite pools in the selected universe that have no trading market yet.
  2. To check its backing before seeding, return to Create and select Details on the saved pool.

The details page tells you whether the SecurityPool can take a market. A Deploy trading pool button means it can; a disabled Initialize trading pool button names the reason it cannot, such as Question ended — pair initialization unavailable. Minting capacity shows the collateral already minted against the pool's ceiling (used / ceiling); your deposit must fit in the difference.

Create and seed the trading pool

  1. Select Deploy trading pool. The form opens with the badge PAIR NOT CREATED and Initialize selected.
  2. Enter 0.01 in ETH amount and 70 in Conditional Yes price.
  3. Read the quote: You provide 0.01 ETH, You receive ≈ LP tokens, 0.01 Invalid, and the Yes shares the pool did not need.
  4. Select Initialize pool and confirm the one transaction that creates and seeds the pool. It reverts if it would mint fewer LP tokens than the quote allows after your slippage tolerance, which you set under Settings.

Check the result

Open the Markets tab. The market is listed with the badge TRADING OPEN and conditional odds of 70% Yes. Then open Portfolio: the market shows your LP tokens, your Invalid, and the spare Yes as separate holdings.

At 70% Yes, the trading pool holds roughly 30 Yes for every 70 No. The No reserve is the larger one because in a constant-product pool the opposite reserve sets the price; Conditional pricing explains why.
