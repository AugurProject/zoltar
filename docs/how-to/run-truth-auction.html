Run a truth auction

Operate a child-pool repair auction after migration without overstating the collateral it can raise.

Confirm eligibility

Verify that the parent migration window ended, the child is still in ForkMigration, and its truth-auction transition has not already started. startTruthAuction(securityPool) either starts the auction transition or atomically finalizes and activates the child when all REP migrated or no collateral repair is required. If that immediate path reverts because continuation carry backing is incomplete, the transition rolls back and the child remains in ForkMigration; reconcile the backing and retry startTruthAuction.

Record the caps

Record the ETH repair target, auctionable child REP, escalation REP contribution, bid window, and settlement parameters. The ETH target and REP inventory are independent caps; demand can leave the auction underfunded.

Start and monitor bidding

  1. Start the auction through the pool forker.
  2. If the call immediately emits TruthAuctionFinalized without starting the auction contract, verify that the child is operational and stop.
  3. If AuctionStarted is emitted, index bids in canonical event order and track accepted ETH, price ticks, cap proximity, and the deadline.
  4. Once a clearing price is already binding, losing bids below it may be refunded before finalization through settleAuctionBids or the auction's public refund path.
  5. Do not promise a fill or final price before finalization.

Finalize and activate

After the bid window, call finalizeTruthAuction(securityPool). The bounded finalization computes the clearing result, transfers the ETH actually raised into child collateral, fixes the REP and allowance conversion rates, conditionally applies a haircut when purchased REP removes a positive escalation allocation, and makes the child operational. If a required continuation lacks aggregate backing, the resumption attempt reverts the ended-auction finalization; reconcile backing and retry finalizeTruthAuction. Finalization does not settle individual bids, credit bidder vaults, or deliver their refunds.

Settle individual bids

After finalization, process bounded pages with settleAuctionBids or claimAuctionProceeds. Winning and partial allocations credit REP-derived ownership and the corresponding allowance to the named bidder vault; losing and unused ETH is refunded. A failed gas-bounded refund push is recorded in pendingEthRefunds, which the bidder can pull with withdrawPendingEthRefund(). These calls are permissionless and may be repeated with new bid indexes until all claims and refunds are settled.

Check the result

Determine whether the child reached complete backing or accepted only the repair supported by auction demand. Weak demand does not block finalization: the child activates with the collateral actually raised. A separate continuation-backing gate applies when the fork carried unresolved escalation; if the child game lacks the aggregate REP required by FORK-08, the transaction reverts. After reconciling carry funding, retry startTruthAuction when the immediate no-auction path rolled back, or retry finalizeTruthAuction when an ended auction remains awaiting finalization. Use resumeForkedEscalationGame() only when the child is already operational and still marked as awaiting continuation. Use the auction explanation for clearing formulas and worked examples.
