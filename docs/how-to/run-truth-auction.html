Run a truth auction

Operate a child-pool repair auction after migration without overstating the collateral it can raise.

Terms: the repair target is the ETH needed to restore the child pool's required collateral; the ETH cap limits accepted repair ETH; the REP cap limits auctionable child REP; the clearing tick is the price tick selected at finalization. In AuctionFinalized, funded == true means demand reached one auction cap and the auction used the uniform-clearing branch. It does not mean that the child received its full repair target. Compare grossAcceptedAttoEth with the ETH cap to determine whether the child was fully repaired; funded == false is the underfunded branch.

Confirm eligibility

Verify that the parent migration window ended, the child is still in ForkMigration, and its truth-auction transition has not already started. startTruthAuction(securityPool) always enters the transition, but it can finalize and activate the child immediately when all REP migrated or no collateral repair is required.

Record the caps

Record the ETH repair target, auctionable child REP, dispute-staked REP contribution, bid window, and settlement parameters. The ETH target and REP inventory are independent caps; demand can leave the auction underfunded.

Bidder preflight

Read maxAttoRepBeingSold(), attoEthRaiseCap(), minBidSizeAttoEth(), auctionStarted(), and finalized(). For each selected tick, read tickToPrice(tick), getTickSummary(tick), and the bidder's balance. A bid is ETH value in attoETH and the tick is the auction's signed price index.

Start and monitor bidding

  1. Start the auction through the pool forker.
  2. If the call immediately emits TruthAuctionFinalized without starting the auction contract, verify that the child is operational and stop.
  3. If AuctionStarted is emitted, index bids in canonical event order and track accepted ETH, price ticks, cap proximity, and the deadline.
  4. Once a clearing price is already binding, losing bids below it may be refunded before finalization through settleAuctionBids or the auction's public refund path.
  5. Do not promise a fill or final price before finalization.

Bid with submitBid(int256 tick) and record BidSubmitted. Never infer an allocation before AuctionFinalized.

Finalize and activate

After the bid window, use previewFinalization(), then call finalizeTruthAuction(securityPool). The bounded finalization computes the clearing result, transfers only ETH actually raised, conditionally applies a haircut when purchased REP removes a positive escalation allocation, and makes the child operational. A resumption attempt reverts finalization when a required continuation lacks aggregate backing; reconcile backing and retry. Finalization does not settle individual bids, credit bidder vaults, or deliver their refunds.

Settle individual bids

After finalization, process bounded pages with settleAuctionBids or claimAuctionProceeds. Winning and partial allocations credit REP backing units and the corresponding coverage commitment to the named bidder vault; losing and unused ETH is refunded. A failed gas-bounded refund push is recorded in pendingEthRefundsAttoEth, which the bidder can pull with withdrawPendingEthRefund(). These calls are permissionless and may be repeated with new bid indexes until all claims and refunds are settled.

Numeric scenarios

Scenario Inputs Result
No auction needed Repair target: 0 ETH; REP sale cap: 100 REP Child activates immediately; AuctionStarted is not emitted.
Cap-bound and fully repaired Repair target/ETH cap: 10 ETH; REP cap: 100 REP; ETH submitted: 14 ETH funded == true; 10 ETH accepted, 4 ETH refunded, and the child has no auction shortfall.
Cap-bound but not fully repaired Repair target/ETH cap: 10 ETH; REP cap: 100 REP; demand reaches the REP cap at a price that accepts 6 ETH funded == true; the REP cap binds, but 6 ETH is accepted and the child retains a 4 ETH impairment.
Underfunded branch Repair target: 10 ETH; REP cap: 100 REP; ETH submitted and accepted: 6 ETH funded == false; qualifying bids receive the REP cap, and the child retains the unraised repair shortfall.

A bidder submits ETH with auction.submitBid{value: bidAttoEth}(tick). Finalization and later bounded bid settlement reconcile accepted ETH, refunds, REP allocation, coverage commitment, and pending refund liability.

Check the result

Determine whether the child reached complete backing by comparing accepted ETH with the repair target, not by reading funded alone. A successfully finalized underfunded auction is complete and leaves the child operational but economically impaired; weak demand alone does not require a retry. Reconcile backing and retry finalization only when the transaction reverted because an escalation continuation lacked aggregate REP backing. Use resumeForkedEscalationGame() only when the child is already operational and still marked as awaiting continuation. Use the auction explanation for clearing formulas and worked examples.

Reconcile the three outcomes

Outcome Evidence Next action
No auction Immediate TruthAuctionFinalized or no repair required Verify child operational and stop.
Cap-bound clearing AuctionFinalized.funded == true; compare grossAcceptedAttoEth with attoEthRaiseCap because REP-cap-first clearing can still leave an ETH shortfall. Settle bids; reconcile accepted ETH, the repair cap, REP sold, and any haircut before calling the child fully repaired.
Underfunded funded == false Record accepted repair and haircut; do not retry merely for weak demand. Full ETH repair is a separate comparison: grossAcceptedAttoEth == attoEthRaiseCap.
