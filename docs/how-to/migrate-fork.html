Migrate through a fork

Select a child branch, then apply the deadline and materialization rule for each position type. Not every claim expires with the child-creation window.

Verify the fork

Confirm the parent universe's fork question, outcome encoding, fork time, valid child identifiers, and the pool's own fork-activation state. Verify that fork activation automatically materialized the parent's deterministic pool migration proxy; it is not a separate migration-window action. A Zoltar fork does not by itself prove that a particular pool has completed its snapshot transition.

Choose the branch

Evaluate outcome evidence independently and choose the intended child universe. Record the exact child identifier. Migration expresses branch support and can have irreversible economic consequences; do not infer the branch from display order or an unverified interface.

Migrate each position

Position or action Time rule Verification
Create a missing Statoblast child pool Must occur within the pool-local eight-week migration window. Confirm the deterministic child identifier, parent pool, and deployed code.
Deploy a Zoltar child universe or split raw Zoltar migration REP No Statoblast eight-week pool deadline; follow Zoltar fork identity, outcome, and balance rules. Verify the child universe and REP token derived from the fork question and selected outcome.
Migrate REP backing units and associated pool-held vault REP backing Use the Statoblast pool-local migration window and REP-backing-unit checks. Compare parent deltas with child REP backing units, coverage commitment, REP, and collateral.
Claim direct own-fork escalation deposits Use the own-fork claim window and require an unpriced child branch. Verify the authenticated depositor, outcome, consumed source claim, and child REP recipient.
Materialize shares into an existing child No expiry after the child exists; creating a missing destination remains deadline-gated. Verify the source entitlement, child share balance, and replay protection.
Settle carried escalation claims Follow the child continuation lifecycle and proof rules rather than treating the claim as ordinary vault migration. Verify the parent snapshot, carry proof, immutable depositor, and settlement state.

FORK-05 is the canonical deadline and post-deadline share-materialization rule.

Reconcile the child

Compare migrated REP, collateral, open interest, REP backing units, coverage commitment, shares, and carried escalation totals with the parent deltas. Expect collateral to follow migration rather than appear in full in every child.

Complete child recovery

After the window, determine whether the child needs a truth auction and whether a paused escalation continuation can resume. Do not reopen normal operation until the child satisfies its lifecycle and backing gates.
