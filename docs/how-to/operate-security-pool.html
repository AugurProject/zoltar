Security-pool operator runbook

Choose the focused operating procedure that matches the pool state, then verify the resulting accounting checkpoint before moving on.

Runbook scope

This runbook is the operator hub for a reviewed deployment. The focused transaction guides below cover individual jobs. Record the chain ID, deployment manifest, pool address, block number, and operator identity before acting. Treat all amounts as attoETH, attoREP, or attoShares exactly as named by the ABI.

Confirm pool identity

Verify the chain, pool address, question, universe, factory relationship, share token, truth auction, price coordinator, WETH, and REP token. Stop if the pool is not part of the reviewed deployment.

Inspect lifecycle state

Determine whether the pool is active, locally resolving, forked, migrating, awaiting repair, or finalized. Do not infer permission from a visible UI control; confirm the exact prerequisites for the intended entrypoint in the contract interaction reference.

Maintain backing

Use the authoritative accounting getters and page active vaults before any backing change. For the exact deposit transaction and checkpoint, follow Fund a security vault; for withdrawal, coverage changes, or unhealthy backing, use the focused procedures below. Do not assume depositing REP cancels an already executable liquidation.

Focused procedures

  • Fund a security vault when a reviewed vault needs REP backing.
  • Change a vault allowance only after a fresh-price preview.
  • Withdraw or redeem after checking lifecycle and accounting state.
  • Respond to an unhealthy vault with a separate liquidation transaction.
  • Respond to a fork and repair a child only after ordinary pool work stops.

Fund a security vault

Read systemState(), repToken(), securityVaults(vault), and the caller's REP balance and allowance. Approve exactly the required amount, call depositRepToVault(attoRepAmount), and verify RepDepositedToVault plus VaultAccountingCheckpoint. Completion means the expected backing units increased and no lifecycle state changed unexpectedly.

Change a vault allowance

A coverage-changing operation uses requestPriceIfNeededAndStageOperation(SetCoverageCommitment, callerVault, amountAttoEth, validForSeconds, proposedRepPerEthPrice, requestedInitialAttoWeth). A fresh price needs no report bounty; the first stale operation supplies coordinator-required msg.value and WETH/REP approvals; a pending-report operation must be submitted by the existing sponsor without opening a second report. Inspect the vault again after callback or immediate execution and require ExecutedStagedOperation plus a new accounting checkpoint before recording success.

Withdraw or redeem safely

For a live REP withdrawal, use the coordinator path while the pool is operational and the local game remains unresolved; inspect the staged operation and its fresh-price guards before execution.

For vault REP redemption after a final outcome, confirm the pool is operational, the vault has positive redeemable REP, and its dispute-staked REP is zero. Any caller may then call redeemRepFromVault(vault); REP is transferred to the named vault and the receipt identifies both caller and vault in RepRedeemedFromVault.

For winning shares, redeemShares() requires an operational pool and final outcome; verify SharesRedeemed. For fees, anyone may call redeemFees(vault) without a lifecycle or finality guard; ETH is paid to the named vault and the receipt may include the fee accounting checkpoints.

For complete sets, redeemCompleteSet(amountAttoShares) requires an operational, unforked pool; it does not require a final outcome. A positive amount requires matching shares, while zero follows the zero-value event, checkpoint, and ETH-call path without a token balance. Verify CompleteSetRedeemed. Stop if a transition, pending operation, or liquidation makes the preview stale.

Advance public transitions

Monitor deadlines and settle-ready states. Public transitions are not evidence of operational readiness until their resulting events and getters agree. Record transaction hashes, the block hash, and the resulting PoolAccountingCheckpoint or lifecycle event.

Respond to unsafe state

If a vault is liquidatable, follow the liquidation guide. If a staged callback failed, follow the recovery guide. If the universe forked, stop ordinary pool work and follow the migration guide.

Completion checklist

  • The chain, manifest, pool, universe, and question match the reviewed deployment.
  • The transaction receipt is final under the deployment's stated finality policy.
  • Expected events and resulting getters agree, including units.
  • No pending price operation, unexpected lifecycle transition, or unaccounted balance remains.
