How-to guide

Seed alternative conditional odds

Use this guide when you call the trading pool contract yourself and want a new trading pool to open at a specific Conditional Yes price rather than 50%.

Before you call initialize

  • Create the trading pool for the SecurityPool with the factory's createPair(securityPool), unless it already exists.
  • Approve the trading pool as an operator on the canonical ShareToken; initialize pulls both deposits from the caller.
  • Make the smaller deposit larger than MINIMUM_LIQUIDITY (1,000 share base units). The trading pool mints that many LP base units to a lock address that no key controls, so you receive the smaller deposit minus 1,000.

The trading pool reference lists every revert condition.

Compute the deposits

You do not need this arithmetic in the UI: the Create market form takes a Conditional Yes price and the router's createPairAndInitializeWithEth computes the deposits, as Your first Trading market shows. Use it only when you call pair.initialize(yesAmount, noAmount, minLiquidity, recipient) directly.

Choose the Conditional Yes price p in basis points with 0 < p < 10,000, and let q be the number of complete sets you created, in share base units.

  • If p ≥ 5,000, deposit all q No and ⌊q × (10,000 − p) / p⌋ Yes.
  • If p < 5,000, deposit all q Yes and ⌊q × p / (10,000 − p)⌋ No.

Both amounts must be nonzero. Conditional pricing works through the 70% example and explains why the larger reserve is the side you are betting against; the units and rounding reference gives the rounding directions.
