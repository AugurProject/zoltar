Advance an escalation game

Submit deposits and advance public transitions without confusing local settlement with fork continuation.

Identify the game

Verify the pool, question, universe, active game address, round, tentative answer, deposit target, deadline, and whether the game is an ordinary game or a child continuation.

Prepare a deposit

Choose an eligible outcome and calculate the accepted REP amount from current state. Confirm approval, balance, recipient compatibility, deadline inclusion margin, and the economic risk of the claim. Do not submit from a stale round snapshot.

Advance the round

Submit the deposit, verify the deposit event and resulting round values, then wait for or execute the next public transition once its deadline and threshold conditions are satisfied. Keep independent evidence for the underlying answer.

Settle or enter fork handling

If the game selects a local winner, settle it and verify claim accounting before withdrawal. If it reaches non-decision, activate the pool fork path and verify the Zoltar fork relationship. Do not treat a paused child continuation as an ordinary unsettled parent game.

Continue after a fork

For child continuations, verify the parent snapshot and carry proof, then finalize any required truth auction. The configured SecurityPoolForker applies a haircut only when purchased REP removes a positive escalation allocation, while the game is paused. Finalization then activates the pool and immediately attempts continuation resumption. Incomplete aggregate backing reverts the entire finalization; reconcile the missing backing and retry finalization rather than calling a separate recovery transition. Use permissionless resumeForkedEscalationGame() only for a pool that is already operational but still has its awaiting-continuation flag. See the proof reference and architecture explanation.
