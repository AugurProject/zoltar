How-to guide

Handle resolution

Once a market's question has ended, swaps and liquidity additions are closed, but every asset you hold still has an exit. Use this guide to turn LP tokens, complete sets, and winning shares back into ETH.

Unwind after resolution

  1. Remove your LP tokens from the trading pool into Yes and No shares (Remove liquidity).
  2. Redeem matching Invalid, Yes, and No as complete sets while the SecurityPool still allows it.
  3. After the market finalizes, redeem the winning share through SecurityPool.redeemShares.

On an Invalid result, Invalid is the only winning share. Yes and No balances, including pool fees earned as LP, are worth nothing once they lose.

In the UI

Open the market. A closed market opens on its Settlement tab.

  • To redeem complete sets, select Complete set, enter the ETH value in Complete-set value to redeem, check You receive ≈, and select Redeem complete sets.
  • Once the market finalizes, a Redeem YES, Redeem NO, or Redeem INVALID option appears for the winning outcome. It redeems your entire balance of that share through this exact SecurityPool. Before finalization the option is absent.

Every settlement transaction is simulated again at the latest block before your wallet opens, and stops if it would now fail.
